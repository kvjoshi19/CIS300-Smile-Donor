About Smile Donor

Smile Donor is a U.S. 501(c)(3) nonprofit founded in 2014 by Rajneek Khillan, PhD. Our mission is to support underprivileged children by improving access to education, care, and basic needs. We partner with trusted grassroots NGOs in India and review each organization closely to make sure donations create real impact. Our team focuses on transparency, clear communication, and strong accountability while working with local partners to strengthen programs and help more children thrive.
