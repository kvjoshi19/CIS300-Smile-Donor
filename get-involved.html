Get Involved

There are several ways you can support Smile Donor and help create opportunities for children in need. Whether you choose to donate, volunteer your time, or help spread awareness, every action makes a meaningful difference. The options below show how different donation amounts directly support specific programs and partner organizations. After reviewing the table, you can click the Contact Us button to take the next step. On the Contact page, you can fill out the form and select whether you want to donate, volunteer, or ask a question, and Smile Donor will reach out to you shortly.

Donation Amount What It Provides Program Area / Organization
$50/month or $600/year Supports a child with disabilities through home-based therapy, personalized education plans, and regular progress monitoring. Special Needs Support — Ganpati Education Society (Himachal Pradesh, India)
$20/month Provides food, clothing, shelter, and education for one child living in an orphanage. Orphanage Support — Vikasha Parishad (Odisha, India)
$500 (one-time) Funds one student’s higher education, including tuition and academic resources. Scholarship Program — Partner Educational Institutes (India)
